## 摘要
位移贴图将精细几何细节编码在紧凑纹理中，简化了曲面创作；但通常需要先将曲面细分为稠密三角形网格才能使用，因而带来高昂的内存开销。近期的无细分方法实现了直接光线追踪。然而，曲面采样、镜面流形搜索和最近点查询等更广泛的几何查询仍较少受到探索。支持这些操作需要曲面包围体、面积估计和法线界，而仅靠标量高度区间无法充分提供这些信息。我们提出一种一阶纹理空间层次结构，无需预先细分即可直接支持这些查询。每个节点存储一个局部仿射高度平面及其保守残差界，从而保留标量最小—最大界会丢弃的空间高度变化。该平面及残差可为相交和距离搜索提供曲面包围体，而平面的高度与斜率则可用于估计采样面积。额外的斜率界支持镜面光路搜索。基于这一表示，我们开发了针对不同查询的遍历、剪枝和采样算法，并将其应用于焦散渲染、位移发光体采样以及曲面偏微分方程（PDE）求解器。实验表明，与预先细分相比，该方法节省了内存；与针对特定查询的基线方法相比，性能有所提升，同时仍能高效进行光线追踪。

## Abstract
Displacement maps encode fine geometric detail in compact textures and simplify surface authoring, but using them typically requires pre-tessellating surfaces into dense triangle meshes, incurring a heavy memory cost. Recent tessellation-free methods enable direct ray tracing. However, broader geometric queries such as surface sampling, specular manifold search, and closest-point queries remain less explored. Supporting these operations requires surface enclosures, area estimates, and normal bounds, which scalar height intervals alone do not fully provide. We present a first-order texture-space hierarchy that unlocks these queries directly without pre-tessellation. Each node stores a local affine height plane and a conservative residual bound, retaining spatial height variation that scalar minimum--maximum bounds discard. The plane and residual provide surface enclosures for intersection and distance searches, while the plane's height and slopes supply area estimates for sampling. Additional slope bounds support specular light-path search. Building on this representation, we develop query-specific traversal, pruning, and sampling algorithms, and demonstrate them in caustics rendering, sampling displaced emitters, and surface partial differential equation (PDE) solvers. We demonstrate memory savings over pre-tessellation and performance improvements over query-specific baselines, while retaining efficient ray tracing.
