## 摘要
大微分同胚变形度量映射（LDDMM）框架提供了多种用于分析形状和图像的技术。表面匹配旨在计算一种非刚性、平滑且可逆的映射，以将一个表面配准到另一个表面。计算得到的变形可能包含刚体运动，这类运动通常并不代表有意义的形状差异，而是由成像模态等外部因素造成的，例如图像采集过程中物体的摆放位置。我们通过引入一种合适的投影，对 LDDMM 框架中的表面匹配方法进行简单扩展，使计算得到的变形能够唯一分解为刚体运动模式（RBM）和去除刚体运动模式后的运动。这样既能分别对各个分量进行有针对性的分析，也有助于保持表面离散化的质量。我们通过三组示例展示该方法：首先以一个小规模示例说明所提方法的效果；随后展示一对心脏几何形状的示例；最后展示一个包含 98 个心脏形状的队列示例。

## Abstract
The large diffeomorphic deformation metric mapping (LDDMM) framework offers numerous techniques for analyzing shapes and images.   Surface matching computes a non-rigid, smooth, and invertible mapping that registers one surface to another.   The computed deformation may contain rigid body motion, which frequently does not represent meaningful shape differences but is due to external factors like imaging modalities, such as the object's positioning during image acquisition.   We present a simple extension to the LDDMM framework's surface matching that uniquely decomposes the computed deformation into rigid body modes (RBM) and RBM-free motion by introducing a suitable projection.   This enables a focused analysis of each component individually and additionally helps to preserve the surface discretization quality. Three sets of examples illustrate our approach.   A small-scale example demonstrates the proposed method's effects, followed by an example using a single pair of heart geometries, and finally an example involving a cohort of 98 heart shapes.
