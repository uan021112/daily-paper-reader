## 摘要
我们研究了冻结的预训练视觉表征与深度表征在结合监督式匹配网络时如何支持稠密光流估计。本文以 TTSFlow 为基础，研究了稠密预测 Transformer（DPT）的特征解码以及一个由粗到细的细化阶段。我们融合冻结的 DINOv2 和 Depth Anything V2 模型提取的特征，并通过可训练的、基于 Transformer 的匹配流水线估计光流。在 FlyingChairs 和 FlyingThings3D 上进行光流训练后，该方法在 Sintel 训练集的 Final pass 上取得 2.37 的端点误差（EPE），而本文报告的 FlowSeek (T) 配置为 2.52。我们参加 RoCo-Spring 2026 的作品 RoCo-29.VSAI 获得了 1.1038 的鲁棒性平衡分数、1.411 的 Spring EPE，以及 4.739 的 RobustSpring 代理 EPE；在列出的十二个参考基线中，其平衡分数低于七个。上述结果体现了完整流水线的性能：该流水线复用冻结的基础模型骨干网络，并执行一个细化阶段。代码见：\url{https://github.com/Visual-and-Spatial-AI-Lab/FFlow}

## Abstract
We investigate how frozen pretrained visual and depth representations support dense optical flow when coupled with a supervised matching network. Building on TTSFlow, this paper studies Dense Prediction Transformer (DPT) feature decoding and one coarse-to-fine refinement stage. Our method fuses features from frozen DINOv2 and Depth Anything V2 models and estimates optical flow through a trainable transformer-based matching pipeline. After optical-flow training on FlyingChairs and FlyingThings3D, it achieves 2.37 endpoint error (EPE) on the Sintel training split, Final pass, compared with 2.52 for the FlowSeek (T) configuration reported here. Our RoCo-Spring 2026 entry, RoCo-29.VSAI, obtains a robustness-balanced score of 1.1038, Spring EPE of 1.411, and RobustSpring proxy EPE of 4.739, with a lower balanced score than seven of the twelve listed reference baselines. These results characterize the complete pipeline, which reuses frozen foundation backbones and performs one refinement stage.
