## 摘要
我们提出一种基于 OWL 感知函数构建的、原理清晰且具有解析形式的框架，用于在单次相机运动过程中分割多个独立运动的刚性物体。OWL 将两种基本视觉运动线索——感知逼近量 $L$ 和感知旋转量 $ω$——编码为相对三维结构的单一闭式表示。该结构的一个关键推论是，仅凭 $L$ 和 $ω$ 即可针对每个图像点解析地恢复相对平移的单位方向 $\hat{t}$。属于同一非旋转刚体的所有点具有相同的 $\hat{t}$，而不同刚体上的点通常会得到不同的取值。这提供了一种自然的分割准则：对逐点估计的 $\hat{t}$ 进行聚类，即可将可见场景划分为独立运动的刚体。在 OWL 的二维复数形式基础上，我们使用四元数推导出适用于单台运动相机的三维框架。该方法可并行计算、不受相机旋转影响，且无需训练数据、深度信息或语义先验。

## Abstract
We present a principled, analytical framework for segmenting multiple independently moving rigid objects during single camera motion, built directly upon the OWL perception function. OWL encodes two fundamental visual motion cues, perceived looming $L$ and perceived rotation $ω$, into a single closed-form representation of relative 3D structure. A key consequence of this structure is that the unit direction of relative translation $\hat{t}$ is analytically recoverable per image point from $L$ and $ω$ alone. All points belonging to a non-rotating rigid body share the same $\hat{t}$, whereas points on different rigid bodies generally yield distinct values. This furnishes a natural segmentation criterion: clustering per-point $\hat{t}$ estimates partitions the visible scene into independently moving rigid bodies. Building on the 2D complex form of OWL, we derive the framework in 3D using quaternions, for a single moving camera. The approach is parallel, camera rotation-invariant, and requires no training data, no depth information, and no semantic priors.
