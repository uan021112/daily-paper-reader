## 摘要
仅使用单个摄像机重建并渲染高保真、可动画且适用于虚拟试穿的虚拟化身，对众多应用至关重要。然而，现有适用于虚拟试穿的人体化身重建方法难以仅凭单目视频或单张图像实现这一目标，主要源于两个问题：（1）现有方法通常使用单层表示服装及其下方的人体，忽略了二者不同的运动特性；（2）单摄像机拍摄的输入往往无法完整覆盖服装和下方的人体。为解决这些挑战，我们提出一种物理集成的双层 GS-Mesh 表示，通过融入二维生成数据，可从单目视频乃至单张图像重建解耦的三维着装人体化身。尽管二维生成方法能够根据给定的姿态序列生成视频序列，却难以保持帧间一致性。我们将生成数据中的帧间不一致性分解为几何不一致性和外观不一致性。基于我们的三维表示，我们确保最终的重建与渲染结果兼具三维一致性和时间连贯性。与依赖多视角重建的现有三维服装迁移方法，以及使用扩散模型进行基于图像的服装编辑、却无法合成新视角或新姿态的大多数二维方法相比，我们的方法仅需单目视频甚至单张图像，即可在虚拟试穿和新姿态动画中呈现视觉上合理的布料动态。这标志着仅凭单个摄像机实现具有逼真布料动态的虚拟试穿迈出了重要一步。

## Abstract
Reconstructing and rendering high-fidelity, animatable, and try-on-ready virtual avatars using only a single camera is crucial for many applications. However, existing try-on-ready human avatar reconstruction methods struggle to achieve this from monocular videos or single images, primarily due to two issues: (1) existing approaches typically use a single layer to represent both garments and the underlying body, ignoring their distinct motion characteristics; and (2) input captured from a single camera often fails to fully cover both the garments and the underlying body. To address these challenges, we propose a physically integrated, double-layer GS-Mesh representation for reconstructing decoupled 3D clothed human avatars from monocular videos or even single images by incorporating 2D generated data. While 2D generation methods can produce video sequences from given pose sequences, they struggle to maintain inter-frame consistency. We decompose the inter-frame inconsistencies in the generated data into geometric and appearance inconsistencies. Based on our 3D representation, we ensure that the final reconstruction and rendering results are both 3D-consistent and temporally coherent. Compared with existing 3D garment transfer methods that rely on multi-view reconstruction and most 2D approaches that use diffusion models for image-based garment editing without novel-view or novel-pose synthesis, our method requires only a monocular video or even a single image while producing visually plausible cloth dynamics during virtual try-on and novel-pose animation. This represents a significant advancement toward virtual try-on with visually plausible cloth dynamics using only a single camera.
