## 摘要
需要获取特定目标信息的机器人系统，常常会受到周围结构或目标自身造成的严重遮挡，导致单个相机视角只能提供部分信息。因此，主动感知必须选择能够逐步揭示未观测区域的视角。然而，传统的局部次优视角（NBV）方法通常依据视角的即时效用进行选择，可能忽视那些初始信息量有限、却能为后续视角提供更丰富观测条件的视角。我们将局部目标感知建模为短时域序列视角规划问题，并提出滚动时域次优视角（RH-NBV）规划器，通过评估未来视角的候选序列，而非孤立视角来进行规划。我们在仿真环境和实体机器人上，使用搭载眼在手上 RGB-D 相机的 UR5e 机械臂，对 RH-NBV 进行评估，并与基于梯度的 NBV 规划器及随机搜索进行比较。实验对象包括在受控遮挡条件下的咖啡杯和 Stanford 兔子，以及存在自然自遮挡的番茄植株。在严重遮挡情况下，RH-NBV 能显著提高目标信息恢复效果：在遮挡最严重的咖啡杯配置中，其感兴趣区域（ROI）覆盖率达到 63.8%，而单步 NBV 基线为 34.2%；其遮挡表面召回率达到 85.5%，而单步 NBV 基线仅为 7.4%。受控的规划时域消融实验表明，这一提升源于多步推理，而非额外计算。对于严重遮挡的目标，RH-NBV 带来的收益最大；当可见性限制较少时，短视的 NBV 方法仍具有竞争力，且计算成本显著更低。

## Abstract
Robotic systems that must acquire information about a specific target often face severe occlusion from sur- rounding structures or from the target itself, such that a single camera viewpoint provides only partial information. Active perception must therefore select viewpoints that progressively reveal unobserved regions. However, conventional local next- best-view (NBV) methods typically select viewpoints according to their immediate utility, which can overlook views that provide limited information initially but enable more informative obser- vations from subsequent viewpoints. We formulated local target perception as a short-horizon sequential view-planning problem and proposed a Receding-Horizon Next-Best-View (RH-NBV) planner that evaluates candidate sequences of future viewpoints rather than isolated views. RH-NBV is evaluated on a UR5e manipulator with an eye-in-hand RGB-D camera in simulation as well as on physical robot against a gradient-based NBV planner, and random search, using a coffee mug and Stanford bunny under controlled occlusion and a tomato plant with natural self-occlusion. Under heavy occlusion, RH-NBV achieves substantially higher target information recovery, reaching 63.8% ROI coverage versus 34.2% and 85.5% occluded-surface recall versus 7.4% for the single-step NBV baselines on the most occluded mug configuration. A controlled horizon ablation shows that this improvement arises from multi-step reasoning rather than additional computation. RH-NBV provides the greatest benefit for strongly occluded targets, while myopic NBV remains competitive and substantially cheaper when visibility is less constrained.
