## 摘要
施工机器人必须为只能部分观测到的建筑构件进行标注。我们发现，若测试时的遮挡移除了场景中的整个区域，而非像训练时那样仅遮挡物体的一部分，点云分割网络并非只是漏掉被遮挡的构件，而是会将其重新标注，且错误会集中到单一的吸收类上。在固定的合成布局中，柱类吸收了多达70%的测试点；在随机化布局中，吸收类转移到楼梯类，在三个训练随机种子下，其预测占比达到真实占比的4.5至5.4倍。基线模型的mIoU降至0.15–0.27（固定布局）和0.36–0.72（随机化布局），单个测试集中最多有269个完整物体消失。为每个点附加14个手工计算的几何特征（GPE），可使mIoU恢复至0.54–0.92，并在每次运行中减少64%–100%的完整物体丢失；这一结果适用于两种布局条件下的PointNet++和一种PTv3风格的Transformer。在真实S3DIS扫描数据上，GPE消除了所有完整墙体丢失（从10个降至0个）。根据预测结果进行的竣工柱体核验显示，GPE将核验正确的视图比例从17%–58%提升至71%–92%。对固定、随机化及真实场景进行的特征置换分析揭示了这一规律：每个类别都会依赖其训练数据中稳定的线索；对于按高度分布的楼板和梁，依赖的是位置，而当墙体和楼梯的位置发生变化时，则依赖其固有形状。就mIoU而言，遮挡增强是一种有力的替代方案，但在所有设置中，其造成的完整物体丢失都多于GPE（例如，25–35个，而GPE为0个）。

## Abstract
Construction robots must label building elements they can only partly see. We show that when occlusion at test time removes whole regions of a scene, rather than parts of an object as in training, point-cloud segmentation networks do not simply miss the hidden elements: they relabel them, and the errors collapse onto a single sink class. With fixed synthetic layouts the column absorbs up to 70% of all test points; with randomized layouts the sink moves to the stair, predicted at 4.5-5.4x its true share across three training seeds. Baseline mIoU falls to 0.15-0.27 (fixed) and 0.36-0.72 (randomized), and up to 269 whole objects vanish in a single test set. Appending 14 hand-computed geometric features per point (GPE) restores mIoU to 0.54-0.92 and removes 64-100% of whole-object losses in every run, for PointNet++ and a PTv3-style transformer in both layout regimes; on real S3DIS scans it removes every whole-wall loss (10 to 0). In an as-built column check computed from the predictions, GPE raises the share of correctly checked views from 17-58% to 71-92%. Feature permutation on fixed, randomized and real scenes explains the pattern: each class relies on whichever cue is stable in its training data, position for height-banded slabs and beams, intrinsic shape for walls and stairs once their position varies. Occlusion augmentation is a strong alternative on mIoU, but in every setting it loses more whole objects than GPE (e.g., 25-35 vs. 0).
