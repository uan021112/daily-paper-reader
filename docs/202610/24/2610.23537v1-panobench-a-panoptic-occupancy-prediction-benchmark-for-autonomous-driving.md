## 摘要
全景占用将三维几何、语义和实例身份统一起来，但由于实例感知标注稀缺，其发展受到限制。现有基准主要提供根据不完整传感器观测重建的语义标签，而全景数据集则存在领域覆盖有限或标注不完整的问题。我们提出 PanoBench，这是一个以摄像头为中心、覆盖合成与真实驾驶场景的全景占用预测基准。该基准通过规范化数据接口和统一评测协议整合了 PanoBench-CarlaOcc、PanoBench-nuScenes 和 PanoBench-KITTI360，并提供带有持久实例身份的多分辨率标注。合成分支从模拟网格中生成完整的全景几何。对于真实世界数据，我们设计了一套新流程：利用几何先验和拓扑约束重建背景，根据多帧多视图图像生成前景网格资产，并将其融合为稀疏全景体素。统一的有效掩码协议评估由时序观测支持的完整物体实例，同时以体素为单位评估背景区域。通过分析观测保真度、几何完整性、空间连续性和时间一致性，我们展示了所得标注相较于现有占用基准的优势。我们在语义占用预测、全景占用预测和 4D 占用预测任务上评测了具有代表性的方法，并研究了体素分辨率、合成到真实场景的迁移以及存储效率。结果揭示了体积式指标与基于射线的指标各自的优势，以及真实世界实例感知预测仍面临的挑战。PanoBench 可通过 https://mias.group/PanoBench 获取。

## Abstract
Panoptic occupancy unifies 3D geometry, semantics, and instance identities, but its development is limited by scarce instance-aware annotations. Existing benchmarks mainly provide semantic labels reconstructed from incomplete sensor observations, while panoptic datasets have limited domain coverage or annotation completeness. We introduce PanoBench, a camera-centric panoptic occupancy prediction benchmark spanning synthetic and real-world driving scenes. It combines PanoBench-CarlaOcc, PanoBench-nuScenes, and PanoBench-KITTI360 through canonical data interfaces and unified evaluation protocols, providing multi-resolution annotations with persistent instance identities. The synthetic branch derives complete panoptic geometry from simulated meshes. For real-world data, a new pipeline reconstructs backgrounds using geometric priors and topology constraints, generates foreground mesh assets from multi-frame multi-view images, and fuses them into sparse panoptic voxels. A unified valid-mask protocol evaluates complete thing instances supported by temporal observations while scoring stuff regions at the voxel level. Analyses of observation fidelity, geometric completeness, spatial continuity, and temporal consistency demonstrate the benefits of the resulting annotations over existing occupancy benchmarks. We benchmark representative methods on semantic occupancy prediction, panoptic occupancy prediction, and 4D occupancy forecasting, and study voxel resolution, synthetic-to-real transfer, and storage efficiency. These results reveal complementary strengths of volumetric and ray-based metrics and persistent challenges in real-world instance-aware prediction. PanoBench is available at https://mias.group/PanoBench.
