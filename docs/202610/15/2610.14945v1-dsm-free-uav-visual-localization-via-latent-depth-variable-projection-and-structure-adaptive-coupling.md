## 摘要
视觉定位使无人机（UAV）能够在全球导航卫星系统（GNSS）不可用的环境中保持绝对定位。一种常见方法是将无人机图像与带地理标记的正射地图进行匹配，并利用数字地表模型（DSM）提供的高程，通过透视-n-点（PnP）方法估计相机位姿。然而，实际应用中往往难以获得准确且及时更新的 DSM。正射—透视几何（OPG）能够在高程变化的场景中实现无 DSM 定位，但现有方法通常通过中间矩阵估计与分解间接估计位姿。为解决这一局限，我们提出潜在深度变量投影（LDVP），将透视视线方向上的未知尺度视为潜在深度。给定旋转后，LDVP 通过解析消去这些深度，并以闭式形式求解相机水平中心，从而将非线性估计问题简化为仅关于旋转的优化问题，无需假设场景为平面即可直接恢复具有度量尺度的水平位置。为提升不同场景结构下的定位鲁棒性，我们进一步提出结构自适应耦合（SAC）：利用单目深度估计推断场景的相对结构；当匹配点并非主要分布于单一平面时选择 LDVP，否则采用 Planar-PnP。我们的局部可观测性分析揭示了场景高程变化如何影响 OPG 的几何约束，为这一自适应策略提供了依据。大量实验表明，LDVP 的性能始终优于现有基于 OPG 的无 DSM 方法；SAC 则进一步提升了其在多种场景中的鲁棒性，并显著缩小了与基于 DSM 的定位方法之间的差距。代码将发布于 https://github.com/UAV-AVL/LDVP-SAC。

## Abstract
Visual localization enables unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) to maintain absolute positioning in GNSS-denied environments. A common approach matches UAV images with geo-tagged orthographic maps and estimates camera pose using Perspective-n-Point (PnP) with elevations from Digital Surface Models (DSMs). However, accurate and up-to-date DSMs are often unavailable in practice. Orthographic-perspective geometry (OPG) enables DSM-free localization in scenes with varying elevations, yet existing methods typically estimate pose indirectly through intermediate matrix estimation and decomposition. To address this limitation, we propose Latent-Depth Variable Projection (LDVP), which treats the unknown scales along perspective viewing rays as latent depths. By analytically eliminating these depths and solving for the horizontal camera center in closed form given the rotation, LDVP reduces nonlinear estimation to a rotation-only problem, enabling direct recovery of metric horizontal position without assuming a planar scene. To improve localization robustness across different scene structures, we further introduce Structure-Adaptive Coupling (SAC), which uses relative scene structure inferred from monocular depth estimation to select LDVP when the matched points are not dominated by a single plane, and Planar-PnP otherwise. Our local observability analysis reveals how scene elevation variation affects the geometric constraints of OPG, providing a basis for this adaptive strategy. Extensive experiments show that LDVP consistently outperforms existing OPG-based DSM-free methods, while SAC further improves robustness across diverse scenes and substantially narrows the gap to DSM-based localization. Code will be released at https://github.com/UAV-AVL/LDVP-SAC.
