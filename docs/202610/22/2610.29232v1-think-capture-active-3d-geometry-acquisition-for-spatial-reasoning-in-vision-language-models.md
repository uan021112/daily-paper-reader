## 摘要
视觉语言模型（VLM）擅长二维识别，但在稀疏视角下往往难以维持一致的三维关系，因此在遮挡和视角变化时容易产生空间幻觉。现有方法通常将所有帧与稠密几何信息融合，带来显著的计算与上下文开销，同时也会促使模型盲目依赖并不完美的估计器。我们提出 Think-Capture，这是一种由查询条件驱动的框架，首先识别任务关键对象，再按需获取其几何信息。该框架通过一个三维工具包将零样本掩码提升至统一坐标系，并利用符号化三维状态抽象，将对象和相机状态压缩为结构化文本。分层组相对策略优化（GRPO）在几何信息有助于判断或可能误导判断的案例上训练 VLM，使其学会何时使用工具提供的证据、何时转而依赖视觉线索。Think-Capture-4B 在 MindCube-Tiny 上达到 54.4%（较其骨干模型提高 21.5 个百分点），在 MMSI-Bench 的位置子集上达到 39.1%（提高 7.9 个百分点），在 ViewSpatial 上达到 42.8%（提高 5.2 个百分点）。该模型在坐标和掩码受到扰动时也保持稳健。完整系统可部署于一块 24 GB GPU 上，并作为异步高层推理器运行于 Agibot G1。代码见 https://github.com/819346675/Think-Capture。

## Abstract
Vision-Language Models (VLMs) excel at 2D recognition but often fail to maintain consistent 3D relations across sparse viewpoints, leading to spatial hallucinations under occlusion and perspective changes. Existing methods commonly fuse all frames with dense geometry, incurring substantial computation and context overhead while encouraging blind reliance on imperfect estimators. We present Think-Capture, a query-conditioned framework that first identifies task-critical objects and then acquires their geometry on demand. A 3D toolkit lifts zero-shot masks into a common coordinate frame, and a Symbolic 3D State Abstraction compresses object and camera states into structured text. Hierarchical Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) trains the VLM on cases in which geometry either helps or misleads, teaching it when to use tool evidence and when to fall back on visual cues. Think-Capture-4B reaches 54.4% on MindCube-Tiny (+21.5 points over its backbone), 39.1% on the MMSI-Bench positional subset (+7.9), and 42.8% on ViewSpatial (+5.2). It also remains robust under coordinate and mask perturbations. The full stack fits one 24 GB GPU and serves as an asynchronous high-level reasoner on an Agibot G1. Our code is available at https://github.com/819346675/Think-Capture.
