## 摘要
自适应机器人感知系统必须在检测器精度、推理延迟和能耗之间取得平衡，尤其是在环境温度较高导致处理器发生热降频时。我们提出 Thermal-Headroom Routing（THR，热余量路由），这是一种轻量级模型选择策略，通过闭式 RC 热模型预测处理器温度，并排除预计会超过降频阈值的检测器配置。我们在七条真实世界感知轨迹、八种环境温度和 20 个随机种子下，对四种 YOLO11 配置进行评估，并将 THR 与延迟感知路由、固定阈值温控器、占空比上限策略以及计算预算匹配的对照方法进行比较。在环境温度为 45–55 摄氏度且推理截止时间严格的情况下，THR 在仿真中消除了热降频；与基于延迟置信度的路由器相比，它将截止时间违约率降低了 3.9–7.5 个百分点，并将每个按时处理帧的能耗降低约 12–13%。另一项采用按截止时间节奏执行、帧间不重叠的实验显示，违约率降低了 6.2–9.5 个百分点。经过调优的温控器或占空比上限策略能够在固定工作点达到与 THR 相当的表现；当环境条件或校准后的热参数与静态策略的调优条件不同时，预测式路由最能发挥作用。在 Apple M5 上进行的测量支持单节点升温近似，但并未验证硬件热降频。研究结果刻画了热预测能够提升资源受限机器人感知效率和及时性的适用条件。

## Abstract
Adaptive robot perception systems must balance detector accuracy, inference latency, and energy consumption, particularly when high ambient temperatures cause processors to thermally throttle. We propose Thermal-Headroom Routing (THR), a lightweight model-selection policy that forecasts processor temperature using a closed-form RC thermal model and excludes detector configurations predicted to cross a throttling threshold. We evaluate THR with four YOLO11 configurations across seven real-world perception traces, eight ambient temperatures, and 20 randomized seeds, comparing it with latency-aware routing, fixed-threshold thermostats, duty caps, and a matched-compute-budget control. At ambient temperatures of 45-55 degrees C and a tight inference deadline, THR eliminates throttling in simulation, reduces deadline misses by 3.9-7.5 percentage points, and reduces energy per on-time frame by approximately 12-13% relative to a latency-belief router. A separate experiment using deadline-paced, nonoverlapping frame execution yields miss-rate reductions of 6.2-9.5 percentage points. A tuned thermostat or duty cap can match THR at a fixed operating point; predictive routing is most useful when ambient conditions or calibrated thermal parameters differ from those used to tune static policies. Measurements on an Apple M5 support the single-node heating approximation but do not validate hardware throttling. The results characterize the conditions under which thermal forecasting improves the efficiency and timeliness of resource-constrained robot perception.
