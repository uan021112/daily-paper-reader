## 摘要
部分可观测性仍然是机器人导航中的一个基本挑战，有限的传感器覆盖范围和遮挡会导致环境中很大一部分未被观测到。现有的场景补全方法主要关注改进不完整建图或重建部分观测的三维结构，但很少研究如何设计场景补全以受益于路径规划等下游任务。在这项工作中，我们提出了一种面向路径规划的三维场景补全框架，该框架超越了纯占用建模，转向一种耦合的几何表述。具体而言，以部分 LiDAR 观测为输入，所提框架联合预测补全后的基于截断无符号距离场（TUDF）的连续几何表示和逐体素占用图。这种耦合表示使网络能够更好地推理障碍物边界和自由空间几何结构。为了充分利用两种表示之间的协同作用，我们引入了一种双向耦合学习方案：TUDF 特征提供稠密几何引导以改进占用重建，而占用特征则反过来提供互补的结构约束，以细化距离场估计。因此，所提网络直接预测完整的占用和 TUDF 表示，使 TUDF 能够无缝集成到轨迹规划中，而无需后处理。在未见环境上的大量实验表明，所提方法持续提升了几何重建质量和下游规划性能。

## Abstract
Partial observability remains a fundamental challenge in robotic navigation, where limited sensor coverage and occlusions leave large portions of the environment unobserved. Existing scene completion methods primarily focus on improving incomplete mapping or reconstructing partially observed 3D structures, but rarely investigate how scene completion can be designed to benefit downstream tasks such as path planning. In this work, we propose a path-planning-oriented 3D scene completion framework that moves beyond pure occupancy modeling toward a coupled geometric formulation. Specifically, given partial LiDAR observations as input, the proposed framework jointly predicts completed Truncated Unsigned Distance Field (TUDF)-based continuous geometric representations and voxel-wise occupancy maps. This coupled representation allows the network to better reason about obstacle boundaries and free-space geometry. To fully exploit the synergy between the two representations, we introduce a bidirectionally coupled learning scheme, where TUDF features provide dense geometric guidance to improve occupancy reconstruction, while occupancy features in turn offer complementary structural constraints that refine distance-field estimation. Consequently, the proposed network directly predicts complete occupancy and TUDF representations, allowing seamless integration of TUDF into trajectory planning without post-processing. Extensive experiments on unseen environments demonstrate that the proposed method consistently improves both geometric reconstruction quality and downstream planning performance.
