## 摘要
失真点云中由表面重建引起的差异，同时受到其内在结构复杂度和信号退化的影响。基于这一观察，我们提出一种无参考点云质量评估（PCQA）策略，用于估计并补偿由复杂度引起的差异分量。我们通过连续重建实现该策略。具体而言，对失真点云进行重建会产生输入与重建结果之间的差异，其中同时包含信号退化和内容复杂度的影响。随后，使用更强的平滑操作再次应用重建算子，得到连续两次重建结果之间的差异，并将其作为复杂度引起的差异分量的近似量。利用该近似量对输入与重建结果之间的差异进行校准。我们将相同策略应用于三维几何图块及其对齐的二维区域，从而能够对所得差异进行局部比较。随后，将校准后的几何差异与投影视图差异融合，得到最终退化分数。两个分支的差异均使用冻结的预训练感知表征进行度量。我们的方法无需针对 PCQA 进行专门训练或微调。在三个公开数据库上的实验表明，所提方法始终优于大多数通用基线方法，且性能与若干全参考指标相当。代码可在 https://github.com/HC-W-whc/CCRD-NR-PCQA.git 获取。

## Abstract
The discrepancy induced by surface reconstruction in a distorted point cloud is jointly influenced by its intrinsic structural complexity and signal degradation. This observation motivates a referencefree point cloud quality assessment (PCQA) strategy that estimates and compensates for the complexity-induced component. We implement this strategy through successive reconstruction. Specifically, reconstructing the distorted point cloud produces an input-toreconstruction discrepancy that combines the effects of signal degradation and content complexity. Reapplying the reconstruction operator with stronger smoothing produces a discrepancy between successive reconstructions, which serves as a proxy for the complexityinduced component. This proxy is then used to calibrate the inputto-reconstruction discrepancy. We apply the same strategy to 3D geometry patches and their aligned 2D regions, enabling local comparison of the resulting discrepancies. The calibrated geometric and projected-view discrepancies are then fused into a final degradation score. For both branches, the discrepancies are measured using frozen pretrained perceptual representations. Our method does not require PCQA-specific training or fine-tuning. Experiments on three public databases show that the proposed method consistently outperforms most generic baselines and achieves performance comparable to several full-reference metrics.The code is available at https://github.com/HC-W-whc/CCRD-NR-PCQA.git.
