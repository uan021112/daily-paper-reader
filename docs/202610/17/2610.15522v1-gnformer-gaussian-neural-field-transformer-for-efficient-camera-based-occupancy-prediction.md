## 摘要
基于相机的占用预测能够为机器人感知提供场景几何与语义理解，并已应用于自动驾驶汽车和无人机感知系统。高斯基元是一种颇具前景的替代方案：它有望仅表示被占用区域，从而将计算资源和表征能力集中在所需位置，减少空旷空间中浪费的计算与内存。然而，现有基于高斯的方法存在基元利用率低的问题：由于训练目标设计不够有效，部分高斯会留在空旷空间；同时，高斯函数的表达能力有限，难以建模复杂形状。为解决这些局限，我们提出一种新颖的三维表示——高斯神经场（Gaussian Neural Field，GNF），能够将体素位置准确映射为语义占用。在 GNF 中，高斯与神经网络共同参数化该场：高斯定义其空间支撑范围并为神经解码提供特征，而神经网络则使占用建模能力超越单独使用高斯函数的水平。我们进一步提出高斯神经场 Transformer（Gaussian Neural Field Transformer，GNFormer），这是一种精简且灵活的方法，可预测 GNF，用于基于相机的占用预测。在 Occ-ScanNet、SSCBench-KITTI-360 和 nuScenes-SurroundOcc 基准测试中，与所比较的单帧方法相比，GNFormer 获得了最高的 IoU 和 mIoU，并实现了最快的推理速度。实验中，GNFormer 分别仅用 256 个和 1024 个高斯即可表示室内和室外场景，所需基元数量远少于以往的基于高斯的方法。代码将予以发布。

## Abstract
Camera-based occupancy prediction provides geometric and semantic scene understanding for robot perception and has been adopted in autonomous vehicle and UAV perception systems. Gaussian primitives offer a promising alternative by potentially representing only occupied regions, thereby concentrating computation and representational capacity where needed and reducing wasted computation and memory in empty space. However, existing Gaussian-based methods suffer from poor primitive utilization, as ineffective training objectives leave some Gaussians in empty space, while the limited expressiveness of Gaussian functions restricts their ability to model complex shapes. To address these limitations, we propose a novel 3D representation, Gaussian Neural Field (GNF), which can accurately map voxel positions to semantic occupancies. In GNF, Gaussians and a neural network jointly parameterize the field, where the Gaussians define its spatial support and provide features for neural decoding, while the neural network enables more expressive occupancy modeling than Gaussian functions alone. We further develop the Gaussian Neural Field Transformer (GNFormer), a streamlined and flexible method that predicts GNFs for camera-based occupancy prediction. Across Occ-ScanNet, SSCBench-KITTI-360, and nuScenes-SurroundOcc benchmarks, GNFormer achieves the highest IoU and mIoU and the fastest inference among the compared single-frame methods. In the experiments, GNFormer can represent indoor and outdoor scenes with only 256 and 1024 Gaussians, respectively, substantially fewer primitives than prior Gaussian-based methods.
