## 摘要
前馈几何模型需要哪些层，才能同时保留相机位姿和稠密三维结构？我们研究了 VGGT、π^3 和 VGGT-Ω 中的层冗余：对 3,018 种剪枝配置，在四个室内和室外数据集上，用七项相机位姿与稠密几何指标进行评分。得到四点发现：(i) 可移除层聚集在两个冗余区域：一个占主导的早期区域和一个较窄的后期区域，而跨越中间层的删除始终更具破坏性。这一反复出现的模式在模型、数据集和指标上均成立，并与文献中常见的从中期到后期冗余的报道形成对比。(ii) 在这些区域内，我们观察到删除两个区间所带来的联合退化约等于它们各自退化之和，从而将剪枝搜索的模型评估次数从 O(L^4) 降至 O(L^2)，其中 L 是聚合器深度。(iii) 我们发现 CKA 为区间退化提供了一种更廉价的基于表示的代理指标，在剪枝质量与校准成本之间提供了实用的折中。(iv) 闭式线性校准无需端到端重训练即可在剪枝后恢复精度。最小二乘分析表明，对特殊 token 和 patch token 使用共享映射通常会造成额外重建损失，这促使我们采用 token 感知的恢复。仅用 100 个校准场景拟合的恢复映射可泛化到留出场景和未见数据集。所得模型将聚合器参数减少最多 44%，同时保持与完整模型相当的精度。代码和实验结果将在项目页面发布：https://xian-bei.github.io/vggt-family-layer-redundancy/

## Abstract
Which layers of a feed-forward geometry model are needed to preserve both camera poses and dense 3D structure? We study layer redundancy in VGGT, $π^3$, and VGGT-$Ω$: 3,018 pruned configurations, scored on seven camera-pose and dense-geometry metrics across four indoor and outdoor datasets. Four findings follow: (i) Removable layers cluster in two redundancy regions: a dominant early region and a narrower late one, while deletions spanning the intervening layers are consistently more disruptive. This recurring pattern holds across models, datasets, and metrics, and contrasts with the middle-to-late redundancy commonly reported in the literature. (ii) Within these regions, we observe that the joint degradation from deleting two intervals is approximately the sum of their individual degradations, reducing the number of model evaluations for pruning search from $O(L^4)$ to $O(L^2)$, where $L$ is the aggregator depth. (iii) We find that CKA provides a cheaper representation-based proxy for interval degradation, offering a practical trade-off between pruning quality and calibration cost. (iv) Closed-form linear calibration recovers accuracy after pruning without end-to-end retraining. A least-squares analysis shows that using a shared map for special and patch tokens generally incurs excess reconstruction loss, motivating token-aware recovery. Recovery maps fitted on just 100 calibration scenes generalize to held-out scenes and unseen datasets. The resulting models reduce aggregator parameters by up to 44% while maintaining accuracy comparable to their intact counterparts. Code and experimental results will be available at our project page: https://xian-bei.github.io/vggt-family-layer-redundancy/
