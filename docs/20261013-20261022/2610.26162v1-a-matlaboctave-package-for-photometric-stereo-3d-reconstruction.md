## 摘要
光度立体模型能够利用在不同光照条件下采集的图像，构建真实物体的三维虚拟副本。该模型的假设限制较为严格，在实际应用中很少能得到完全满足。事实上，观测物体的表面可能偏离理想条件，而场景几何结构通常也只是对模型所假设情形的近似。此外，真实世界中的表面可能存在反射现象，从而导致重建结果出现失真。本文介绍 Matlab 软件包 \ttt{\small ps3d}~\cite{cdfrv21} 的修订版本，该软件包旨在实现不同条件下的光度立体模型。新版软件包包含多项扩展，其中最重要的是新增了一种用于处理物体表面反射现象的替代模型。此外，该软件包还实现了用于分析数据集是否符合模型假设的专门度量，并兼容 GNU Octave。用户可借助该工具，在不同观测配置下测试模型，也可使用合成数据或实验数据测试存在反射时的情形。

## Abstract
The photometric stereo model allows the construction of 3D virtual replicas of real objects from images acquired under different light conditions. The assumptions of this model are strongly restrictive and are seldom fully satisfied in applications. Indeed, the surface of the observed objects may deviate from ideal conditions, and the geometry of the scene is often only an approximation of the one assumed by the model. Additionally, real-world surfaces may present reflection phenomena, which introduce distortions in reconstructions. In this paper, we present a revised version of the Matlab package \ttt{\small ps3d}~\cite{cdfrv21}, developed to implement the photometric stereo model under various conditions. The new package contains various extensions, the most notable one is the presence of an alternative model to address the presence of a reflective object surface. In addition, it implements particular metrics to analyze the compliance of a dataset to model assumptions, and it is compatible with GNU Octave. The tool allows users to test the model under different observation configurations, also in presence of reflection, using either synthetic or experimental data.
