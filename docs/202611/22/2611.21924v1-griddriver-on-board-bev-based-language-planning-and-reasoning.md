## 摘要
基于鸟瞰图（BEV）条件的大语言模型（LLM）能够实现语言交互式驾驶，但要在车载环境中部署，必须在自回归语言生成的同时实现高效感知与连续规划。我们提出 GridDriver，这是一种高效的 BEV 条件 LLM 框架，融合了轨迹预测、结构化驾驶指标、自然语言导航、驾驶解释和场景问答。轻量级时空 BEV 感知与空间结构化词元为共享 LLM 提供场景上下文；与此同时，逐帧调度机制将轨迹预测与异步语言生成交错执行。GridDriver 在公开基准上取得了具有竞争力的表现：在 NAVSIM 上达到 87.9% 的 PDMS，在 nuScenes-QA 上达到 57.6% 的准确率，且规划与问答共用同一检查点。空间表示分析表明，与 Q-Former 压缩相比，该方法能够更好地保留几何信息，并提升轨迹预测效果。该系统的独立规划延迟为 32.7 毫秒，在车载 ROS 2 流水线中达到每秒 9.4 次规划。我们还使用自采数据记录评估了规划性能，并展示了系统在研究车辆上的开环车载运行。通过公开基准和真实世界评估，我们分析了基于 BEV 的方法的优势与局限。

## Abstract
Bird's-Eye-View (BEV)-conditioned large language models (LLMs) enable language-interactive driving, but onboard deployment requires efficient perception and continuous planning alongside autoregressive language generation. We present GridDriver, an efficient BEV-conditioned LLM framework combining trajectory prediction, structured driving indicators, natural-language navigation, driving explanations, and scene question answering. Lightweight spatio-temporal BEV perception and spatially structured tokens provide scene context to a shared LLM, while frame-wise scheduling interleaves trajectory prediction with asynchronous language generation. GridDriver achieves competitive performance on public benchmarks, reaching 87.9% PDMS on NAVSIM and 57.6% accuracy on nuScenes-QA with a shared planning and QA checkpoint. Spatial representation analyses demonstrate improved geometric information retention and trajectory prediction compared with Q-Former compression. The system achieves 32.7 ms standalone planning latency and 9.4 plans/s in an onboard ROS 2 pipeline. We further evaluate planning on our own data recordings and demonstrate open-loop onboard operation on a research vehicle. Across public benchmarks and real-world evaluations, we identify strengths and limitations of the BEV-based approach.
