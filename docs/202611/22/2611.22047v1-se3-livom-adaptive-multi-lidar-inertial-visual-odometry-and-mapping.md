## 摘要
本文介绍了 SE(3)-LIVOM，该系统在 IROS 2026 的 COMFORT 挑战赛中夺冠。该挑战要求在 GrandTour 数据集中估计一台足式机器人的轨迹，数据集场景包括倒塌建筑、施工现场和雪地。SE(3)-LIVOM 使用两台激光雷达、一台相机和一个 IMU 实时运行。其前端将 SE(3)-LIO 扩展至多台激光雷达和一台相机。自适应降采样使单次扫描的处理时间在不同场景内及场景间均保持在约 35 毫秒。在线后端通过对子地图进行平面束调整，并结合增量式位姿图来校正漂移。对于所有场景，系统均采用同一套配置，在六个测试场景上取得了 1.40 厘米的平均 ATE。代码见 https://github.com/url-kaist/se3-livom-comfort。

## Abstract
This paper presents SE(3)-LIVOM, the overall winner of the COMFORT Challenge at IROS 2026. The challenge asks for the trajectory of a legged robot in the GrandTour dataset, whose scenes include collapsed buildings, construction sites and snowfields. SE(3)-LIVOM runs in real time on two LiDARs, one camera and an IMU. Its frontend extends SE(3)-LIO to multiple LiDARs and a camera. Adaptive downsampling keeps the time per scan near 35 ms within and across scenes. An online backend corrects drift with a plane bundle adjustment over submaps and an incremental pose graph. With a single configuration for all scenes, the system reaches an average ATE of 1.40 cm on the six test scenes. The code is available at https://github.com/url-kaist/se3-livom-comfort.
