## 摘要
机器人的动作会改变其下一次交互时的热条件：释放后保留的热量、随物体携带的热量或储存在表面下方的热量，即使当前温度相近，也可能改变后续接触的结果。训练轨迹必须捕捉这些效应如何将先前动作与后续观测及任务结果联系起来。我们提出 TRR（热机器人渲染器，Thermal Robot Renderer），它为接触双方附加空间分布且按深度解析的热状态，通过接触、传输和分离更新局部热交换，并在统一的执行时钟上生成同步的 RGB—热成像（RGBT）观测和针对具体事件的任务检查。受控干预表明，保留热量、空间传输和内部蓄热为何会影响后续接触结果。基于相同的状态，TRR 生成一个包含六项任务的成对基准，其中有 2,224 对静态—动态样本，即 4,448 条轨迹，包含同步的双视角观测、状态和动作。在专门的学习语料中，利用可见的热历史可将安全接触点选择率从 75.0% 提高至 91.7%；在采用新的加热流程并共享反馈执行机制的条件下，利用动态历史进行训练可使模拟中的任务完成率提高 50 个百分点。我们展示了 MuJoCo 和 Isaac Sim 上的 RGBT 观测适配器，报告了包含十种已准备机器人配置和六种已展示相机视角的工程资源清单，并介绍了开发者工作流，该工作流可将现有机器人场景转化为导出的 RGBT 记录。第 6 节所述的基准、工作流和平台资源构成了计划公开发布的首批内容。演示和资源更新可在项目页面获取。

## Abstract
A robot's actions change the thermal conditions of its next interaction: heat retained after release, carried with objects or stored beneath the surface can change a later contact's outcome despite similar current temperatures. Training trajectories must capture how these effects connect earlier actions to later observations and task outcomes. We present TRR, a \emph{Thermal Robot Renderer} that attaches spatial and depth-resolved thermal state to both contact partners, updates local heat exchange through contact, transport and separation, and generates synchronized RGB--thermal (RGBT) observations and event-local task checks on one execution clock. Controlled interventions show why retained heat, spatial transport and internal storage matter for subsequent contact outcomes. From the same state, TRR produces a paired six-task benchmark of 2,224 static--dynamic pairs, that is, 4,448 trajectories with synchronized dual-view observations, states and actions. Visible thermal history raises safe contact-site selection from 75.0\% to 91.7\% on a dedicated learning corpus, and training on dynamic histories raises simulated transfer completion by 50 percentage points under a new heating procedure with shared feedback execution. We demonstrate RGBT observation adapters on MuJoCo and Isaac Sim, report an engineering inventory of ten prepared robot configurations and six demonstrated camera viewpoints, and describe a developer workflow that carries an existing robot scene to exported RGBT records. The benchmark, the workflow and the platform resources described in Section 6 form the first items of a planned public release. Demonstrations and resource updates are available on the project page.
