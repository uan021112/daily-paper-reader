## Abstract
Streaming 3D reconstruction must preserve geometry under limited memory, yet STAC's fixed-resolution spatial key-value (KV) cache grows as scenes expand. We propose STAC++, a plug-and-play, training-free framework that compresses and reuses spatial memory for long-term reconstruction in unbounded scenes. Adaptive Spatial KV Cache (ASKC) organizes KV states into an octree with adaptive resolutions across regions and layers. Response-Aware Cache Compression (RACC) guides KV merging by measuring attention-output changes, balancing storage savings with geometric preservation. Temporal Context Refinement (TCR) reuses cached intermediate features and recomputes only a short backbone suffix to refine historical predictions with later observations. Integrated into four pretrained streaming backbones, STAC++ reduces spatial memory consumption while maintaining competitive reconstruction quality on long sequences and large scenes, with further quality gains from refinement at limited computational overhead. The code will be made publicly available upon acceptance.
