## Abstract
We introduce Tracktail, a novel architecture designed to efficiently track query points in 3D from multiple calibrated views without any depth input. Our architecture separates the task as a per-camera video encoding of features and a per-query decoder. Notably, camera cross-attention is only performed across queries in the decoder, allowing for efficient inference (7 FPS and less than 4 GB memory for 10 views and 100 query points). We further introduce a per-camera scene scale computed from camera geometry, which allows us to normalize inputs and predictions appropriately across a vast variety of dataset scales. We train on a synthetic Multi-View Kubric dataset as well as a novel Multi-View Point Odyssey benchmark dataset, which consists of synthetic renders of humans and animals across 499 frames. Tracktail achieves state-of-the-art results on the Panoptic Studio, DexYCB, and Multi-View Kubric benchmarks, outperforming methods that rely on estimated depth. Tracktail also demonstrates a strong ability to maintain accurate tracking over longer ranges compared to existing methods. Our code, data, and weights are publicly available: https://ai-hhmi.github.io/tracktail/
