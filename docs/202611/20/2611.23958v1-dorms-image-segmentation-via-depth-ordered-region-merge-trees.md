## 摘要
我们提出 DORMS（用于分割的深度有序区域合并），这是一种基于单目深度图进行图像分割的纯算法方法，在分割过程中无需神经网络或学习得到的参数。输入深度图由现成的单目深度估计器提供。我们通过确定性的并查集扫描构建深度有序合并树，并结合连通分量面积、深度持久性和空间接触约束来引导区域合并。随后，根据指定的目标区域数量进行阈值搜索，以确定分割粒度；同时，通过全局深度去趋势处理，提升局部几何结构的分离效果。在 COCO Panoptic、Cityscapes 和 ADE20K 上开展的大量实验表明，与经典分割基线相比，DORMS 的性能始终有所提升；与零样本神经分割模型相比，也展现出有竞争力的表现。值得注意的是，在 Cityscapes 上，DORMS 的表现优于所有参与评估的零样本模型；在 COCO 和 ADE20K 上，其表现也可与若干方法相媲美。这些结果彰显了纯算法几何推理在无需学习型分割模型的情况下实现图像分割的潜力。

## Abstract
We introduce DORMS (Depth-Ordered Region Merging for Segmentation), a purely algorithmic approach to image segmentation from monocular depth maps, requiring no neural networks or learned parameters in the segmentation process. An off-the-shelf monocular depth estimator provides the input depth maps. Our method constructs depth-ordered merge trees through a deterministic union-find sweep, combining component area, depth persistence, and spatial contact constraints to guide region merging. A threshold search determines the segmentation granularity according to a specified target region count, while global depth detrending improves the separation of local geometric structures. Extensive experiments on COCO Panoptic, Cityscapes, and ADE20K demonstrate consistent improvements over classical segmentation baselines and competitive performance against zero-shot neural segmentation models. Notably, our method outperforms all evaluated zero-shot models on Cityscapes, while remaining competitive with several approaches on COCO and ADE20K. These results highlight the potential of purely algorithmic geometric reasoning for image segmentation without learned segmentation models.
