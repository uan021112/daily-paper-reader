## 摘要
在经过标定的相机阵列中，让每台相机都运行一个单目网格估计器，重建出的人体却彼此相差一米甚至更远。原因在于几何关系：每个估计结果所处的深度，取决于网络所假定的焦距，而非相机的实际焦距。因此，其误差几乎完全沿自身的视线方向，而标定信息能精确给出这一方向。为解决这一问题，我们提出射线—横向一致性（Ray-Lateral Agreement，RLA），其核心原则是：相信射线，而非深度。RLA 首先沿每个估计结果自身的视线移动该结果，从而消除深度偏差，同时保持形状不变。随后，它逐点融合各视角，并采用高斯误差模型，其精度随各自的视线方向而定。最大似然解具有闭式形式，在平均融合与中点三角测量之间平滑过渡，并能在三角测量存在奇异性的区域保持良好的条件数。鲁棒性是更难解决的问题，因为正确的视角沿其视线方向也会彼此不一致。任何基于完整残差构造的权重，都会同时惩罚优质视角与劣质视角。相比之下，射线—横向一致性在每个点上分别评估每个视角，只依据残差在该视角自身视线横向上的分量进行评分。我们在多个摄像头的公开基准数据集上评估了 RLA。在两台、三台和四台摄像头配置，以及三种逐视角姿态估计器下，RLA 的最坏情况误差均持续低于所有三角测量基线。

## Abstract
Put a monocular mesh estimator behind every camera of a calibrated rig, and the reconstructed bodies land a meter or more apart. The cause is geometric: each estimate sits at the depth implied by a focal length the network assumed, not the one the camera has. Its error therefore points almost entirely along its own viewing ray, and the calibration tells us that direction exactly. To address this, we introduce the Ray-Lateral Agreement (RLA), which follows one rule: trust the ray, not the depth. RLA first slides every estimate along its own rays, which removes the depth bias and leaves the shape untouched. It then fuses the views one point at a time, under a Gaussian error whose precision follows each viewing ray. The maximum-likelihood solution is closed-form, interpolates between averaging and midpoint triangulation, and stays well conditioned where triangulation is singular. Robustness is the harder half, because correct views also disagree along their rays. Any weight built on the full residual therefore punishes good views together with bad ones. Ray-lateral agreement instead scores every view, at every point, by its residual across that view's own ray alone. We evaluated RLA on multi-camera public benchmark datasets. Across two, three, and four cameras and three per-view pose estimators, RLA consistently achieves lower worst-case error than all triangulation baselines.
