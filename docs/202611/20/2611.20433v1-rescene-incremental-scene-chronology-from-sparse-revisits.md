## 摘要
场景编年旨在重建跨越数小时或数天的多次访问中的场景，在此期间，物体的位置、是否存在或形状都可能发生变化。这类重建必须保留每个早期状态，但每次重访通常只能获得少量图像。为每次访问存储完整模型会重复存储未发生变化的场景，而更新单一共享模型则会改变早期状态。我们提出 ReScene，以不可变的紧凑更新来存储每次访问，因此只需按顺序应用已存储的更新，即可精确恢复任意早期访问的状态，无须保留过去的图像。为定位每次更新，ReScene 利用合成权重，将前一时刻重建结果与新图像之间的差异归因于各个高斯基元。这种归因有助于抑制过时的高斯基元并细化持续存在的基元，同时利用学习得到的多视图深度初始化新显现的表面。我们还推出 Multi-State 基准，其中包含非刚性形变和部分材质移除场景，以及具有真实几何标注的合成场景。与顺序 3DGS 检查点存储相比，ReScene 在取得更高质量的同时，将未压缩历史数据的存储量减少了 2.5 至 4.8 倍。在 PSNR 相近的情况下，与顺序 ChronoGS 相比，ReScene 还将前景深度误差降低了 3.2 倍，表明准确的渲染并不必然意味着几何准确。代码与基准数据集见 https://waychin-weiqin.github.io/ReScene/。

## Abstract
Scene chronology aims to reconstruct a scene across visits separated by hours or days, during which objects may change in position, presence or shape. Such reconstruction must retain every earlier state, yet each revisit often provides only a few images. Storing a complete model for each visit duplicates the unchanged scene, whereas updating a single shared model alters earlier states. We present ReScene, which stores each visit as an immutable compact update, so that any earlier visit can be recovered exactly by applying the stored updates in order, without retaining past images. To localise each update, ReScene attributes the disagreement between the preceding reconstruction and the new images to individual Gaussians through their compositing weights. This attribution guides the suppression of outdated Gaussians and the refinement of persistent ones, while newly visible surfaces are initialised from learned multi-view depth. We also introduce Multi-State, a benchmark of non-rigid deformation and partial material removal, including synthetic scenes with ground-truth geometry. ReScene achieves quality higher than sequential 3DGS checkpointing with 2.5--4.8$\times$ less uncompressed history storage. At comparable PSNR, it also reduces foreground depth error by 3.2$\times$ relative to sequential ChronoGS, showing that accurate rendering does not necessarily imply accurate geometry. Code and benchmark are available at https://waychin-weiqin.github.io/ReScene/.
