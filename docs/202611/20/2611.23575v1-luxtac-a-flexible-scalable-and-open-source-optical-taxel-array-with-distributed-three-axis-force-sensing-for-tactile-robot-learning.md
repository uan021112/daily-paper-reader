## 摘要
低成本触觉阵列让触觉成为机器人学习中的实用输入，但压阻式设计只能感知压力，而能够分辨剪切力的相机式传感器又过于笨重，无法铺设在夹爪表面。为解决这一问题，我们提出 LuxTac：一种柔性、可扩展的光学触觉单元阵列，厚约 3.5 mm，由现成部件制成。每个触觉单元均为数字式 RGBC 光数字转换器，置于半透明白色硅胶层下方，并由不透明黑色薄膜覆盖，因此，局部压入深度增加时，背向散射的 LED 光强会单调下降。同一块电路板可搭配两种可互换的凝胶组件：一种用于法向力感知；另一种带有彩色侧壁，可通过返回光的颜色编码局部剪切力，且无需相机、磁体或凸点覆盖层。据我们所知，LuxTac 是首个能够在单个触觉单元层面分辨剪切力的光学触觉阵列。我们使用自动化 CNC 压头和六轴力/扭矩传感器对其进行表征。法向力组件在 104 mN 至至少 5.8 N 的范围内呈线性响应（R² = 0.999），重复性误差为 2.0%，迟滞为 5.9%；归一化后，各触觉单元之间的差异为 6%–10%。使用剪切力组件时，通过单元级模型估算每个触觉单元处的三轴力；在三种压头形状和九次留出测试中，所有单元估算值的总和在 x/y/z 方向上的 R² 分别达到 0.96/0.96/0.92，单个触觉单元的估算值则分别达到 0.92/0.93/0.94，剪切方向误差中位数为 4.8 度。在动作分块 Transformer（Action Chunking Transformer）策略中加入 LuxTac 压力读数后，插销入孔任务的成功率较仅使用视觉的基线显著提高。设计文件、代码和视频见 https://luxtac.github.io。

## Abstract
Low-cost tactile arrays have made touch a practical input for robot learning, but piezoresistive designs sense only pressure, while camera-based sensors that also resolve shear are too bulky to tile over a gripper. To solve this problem, we present LuxTac, a flexible, scalable optical taxel array, about 3.5 mm thick and built from off-the-shelf parts. Each taxel is a digital RGBC light-to-digital converter under a translucent white silicone layer capped by an opaque black film, so the back-scattered LED light falls monotonically with local indentation depth. The same board accepts two interchangeable gel cartridges: a plain one for normal sensing, and one with colored walls that encodes the local shear in the color of the returned light, with no camera, magnets, or bump overlay, making LuxTac, to our knowledge, the first optical taxel array to resolve shear at individual taxels. Characterized with an automated CNC indenter and a six-axis force/torque sensor, the normal cartridge responds linearly to force ($R^2$ = 0.999) from 104 mN to at least 5.8 N, with 2.0% repeatability, 5.9% hysteresis, and a taxel-to-taxel variation of 6-10% after normalization. With the shear cartridge, per-taxel models estimate the three-axis force at every taxel; across three indenter shapes and nine held-out sessions, the summed estimates reach $R^2$ = 0.96/0.96/0.92 in x/y/z, each taxel's estimate 0.92/0.93/0.94, and the median shear-direction error is 4.8 degrees. Adding LuxTac pressure readings to an Action Chunking Transformer policy substantially improves peg-in-hole success over a vision-only baseline. Design files, code, and videos are available at https://luxtac.github.io.
