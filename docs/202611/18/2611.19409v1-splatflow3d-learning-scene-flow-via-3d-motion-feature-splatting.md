## 摘要
考虑到三维物体在较短帧间隔内的运动连续性与稳定性，在场景流估计中引入多帧设置有望解决遮挡问题。本文提出双帧骨干网络 SimpleFlow3D，通过分层构建三维运动特征，以先解耦、后耦合的方式整合来自图像平面的稠密信息与来自深度维度的稀疏信息。SimpleFlow3D 以极少的参数取得了当前最优（SOTA）结果。我们还提出一种权重共享的多帧框架，将前一帧的三维运动特征 splat 到当前估计过程中。该框架通过构建伪输入，统一了首帧与非首帧的推理过程。结合所提出的框架与骨干网络后，新的多帧方法 SplatFlow3D 进一步推动了当前最优水平。与双帧骨干网络相比，它取得了更低的误差；与现有方法相比，它所需的参数更少。在 KITTI 和 Spring 数据集上，SplatFlow3D 均在所有已发表和已提交的方法中排名第一。实验表明，SplatFlow3D 提升了遮挡处理能力、非刚性运动估计能力，以及对真实场景的泛化能力。代码见 https://github.com/wwsource/SplatFlow3D。

## Abstract
Considering the motion continuity and stability of 3D objects over short inter-frame intervals, introducing multi-frame settings in scene flow estimation can potentially address occlusion. In this paper, we propose a two-frame backbone, SimpleFlow3D, which integrates dense information from the image plane and sparse information from the depth dimension in a decoupling-followed-by-coupling manner by hierarchically constructing the 3D motion feature. SimpleFlow3D achieves SOTA results with minimal parameters. We also propose a weight-sharing multi-frame framework to splat the previous 3D motion feature to the current estimation process. The framework unifies the first and non-first frame inference by constructing pseudo inputs. After combining the proposed framework and backbone, the newly born multi-frame method SplatFlow3D further advances the state of the art. It achieves lower errors than the two-frame backbone, with fewer parameters than current methods. On both KITTI and Spring, SplatFlow3D ranked 1st over all published and submitted methods. Experiments show that SplatFlow3D improves occlusion handling, non-rigid motion estimation, and generalization to real-world scenes. Code is available at https://github.com/wwsource/SplatFlow3D.
