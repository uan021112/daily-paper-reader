## 摘要
本文提出一种具备深度感知能力的路径规划与碰撞规避框架，用于使护理辅助机器人在存在低矮障碍物的杂乱环境中安全导航。传统的基于 LiDAR 的导航系统虽能准确地进行地图构建与定位，却无法检测透明或低矮障碍物；而基于视觉的系统则容易受到噪声和遮挡的影响。所提出的框架融合 RGB-D 深度感知、基于规则的障碍物规避方法以及 NavfnROS 全局路径规划器，引导机器人从起点移动至目标点。该方法部署于配备 Kinova Gen3 机械臂的 Husky A200 机器人上，构成完整的护理辅助机器人系统。该系统采用多层架构，可在 ROS-Gazebo 环境中实现全局规划、感知融合和实时控制。在杂乱环境模拟场景中的实验评估表明，与单传感器基线方法相比，该系统的成功率最高提升 40%，最小安全间距提升 35%。此外，真实环境中的实验结果进一步验证了该方法可作为一种计算效率高且注重安全的自主医疗机器人导航方案。

## Abstract
This paper presents a depth-aware path planning and collision avoidance framework for the safe navigation of robotic nursing assistants in cluttered environments with low-height obstacles. Conventional LiDAR-based navigation systems, while accurate for mapping and localization, fail to detect transparent or low-height obstacles, whereas vision-based systems are prone to noise and occlusion. The proposed framework integrates RGB-D depth sensing, rule-based obstacle avoidance, and the NavfnROS global path planner to drive the robot from an initial point to a goal point. The proposed approach is implemented on the Husky A200 robot, equipped with a Kinova Gen3 arm, to form a complete setup for a robotic nursing assistant. This system features a multi-layer architecture for global planning, perception fusion, and real-time control in the ROS-Gazebo environment. Experimental evaluations in a simulated cluttered environment demonstrate up to 40\% higher success rate and 35\% improvement in minimum clearance compared with single-sensor baselines. Furthermore, real-world experimental results validate the proposed approach as a computationally efficient and safety-aware navigation solution for autonomous healthcare robots.
