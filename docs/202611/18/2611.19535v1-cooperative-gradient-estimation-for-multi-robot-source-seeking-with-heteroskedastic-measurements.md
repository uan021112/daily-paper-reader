## 摘要
本文提出了一种新方法，利用异构协作移动机器人团队定位三维标量场的源。每个机器人测量其所在位置的场强，并在团队内共享信息。本文明确考虑测量噪声；由于各机器人传感精度不同，数据呈现异方差特性。所提出的框架将基于加权质心 Voronoi 镶嵌的编队策略与加权最小二乘估计器相结合：该编队策略定义在球面上，并以传感器噪声方差为参数；加权最小二乘估计器则用于计算编队中心处的场梯度。通过在编队设计和估计过程中显式考虑传感器异构性，该方法能够在含噪测量条件下实现稳健的梯度估计。具体而言，我们从解析角度表明，该构造近乎最优。我们以闭式形式推导出梯度估计的协方差，为球面上任意编队所能达到的估计方差给出下界，并证明所提出的镶嵌在渐近意义下满足达到该下界的条件。随后，利用估计得到的梯度，通过梯度上升策略引导机器人团队朝源位置移动。仿真结果表明，与其他策略相比，所提出的方法具有良好效果，并展现出在机器人发生故障时在线重新配置的固有能力，从而增强了其适应性。

## Abstract
This paper proposes a novel approach for localizing the source of a three-dimensional scalar field using a team of heterogeneous cooperative mobile robots. Each robot measures the field intensity at its location and shares the information within the team. Measurement noise is explicitly considered, and heterogeneity arises from robot-dependent sensing accuracies, leading to heteroskedastic data. The proposed framework combines a weighted centroidal Voronoi tessellation-based formation strategy, defined on a spherical surface and parameterized by sensor noise variances, with a Weighted Least Squares estimator to compute the field gradient at the formation center. By explicitly accounting for sensor heterogeneity in both formation design and estimation, the method achieves robust gradient estimation under noisy measurements. In particular, we show analytically that this construction is nearly optimal. The covariance of the gradient estimate is derived in closed form, providing a lower bound on the attainable estimation variance for any formation on the sphere, and the proposed tessellation is shown to asymptotically satisfy the conditions under which this bound is attained. The estimated gradient is then exploited within a gradient-ascent strategy to guide the team toward the source location. Simulation results demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed approach compared to alternative strategies and highlight its intrinsic ability to reconfigure online in response to robot failures, thereby enhancing its adaptability.
