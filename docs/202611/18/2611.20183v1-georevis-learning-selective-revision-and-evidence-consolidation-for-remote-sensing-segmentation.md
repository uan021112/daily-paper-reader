## 摘要
近期方法尝试检查并修订掩码，以缓解语言引导的遥感分割中由复杂空间关系导致的指代歧义和定位错误。然而，这些方案往往会造成“修订退化”：修补缺失区域时，可能在此前正确的区域引入错误。即便全局表现更好的掩码，也可能遗漏较差掩码中包含的正确区域。我们将正确目标证据散落在不完美掩码中的现象称为“证据碎片化”。在此阶段，模型生成的所有掩码都并非最优。为此，我们提出 GeoReVis，将执行反馈引导的修订、候选选择与跨掩码证据整合统一起来。GeoReVis 从执行后确实能改善分割的实际操作中学习细粒度修订，并依据真实候选掩码之间的质量比较学习选择更优掩码，从而降低修订过程中的退化风险。然而，所选的完整掩码仍可能丢弃有用区域。因此，GeoReVis 会保留各轮中的所有候选掩码，将它们在空间上的分歧划分为可组合区域，并结合完整指令与上下文对这些区域进行联合重建。由此，无论互补且碎片化的证据来自整体质量如何的候选掩码，都能得到复用，从而提升分割效果。我们进一步构建了一套数据流水线，用于生成细粒度的多轮分割轨迹，并从模型自身的输出中提取修订操作、候选选择和区域组合的监督信号，使训练不再局限于整幅掩码的 IoU 监督。我们还提出 ReVis-Eval，这是一种用于评估多轮修订及跨轮证据整合准确性的基准测试协议。在 ReVis-Eval 以及 5 个遥感推理和指代分割基准上的实验，验证了 GeoReVis 的有效性。

## Abstract
Recent methods try to inspect and revise masks to mitigate referential ambiguity and localization errors caused by complex spatial relations in language-guided remote sensing segmentation. However, these solutions often lead to Revision Degradation: revising missing regions may introduce errors into previously correct ones. Even a globally better mask can omit correct regions found in inferior masks. We term this Evidence Fragmentation: correct target evidence is scattered across imperfect masks. All masks generated by the model at this time are suboptimal. We propose GeoReVis, which unifies revision guided by execution feedback, Candidate Selection, and Cross-Mask Evidence Consolidation. It learns fine-grained revision from real actions that improve segmentation when executed and learns to select a better mask through quality comparisons between actual candidates, reducing the risk of degradation during revision. The selected whole mask can still discard useful regions. GeoReVis therefore retains all candidates across rounds, partitions their spatial disagreements into composable regions, and jointly reconstructs regions conditioned on the full instruction and context. This makes complementary and fragmentary evidence reusable regardless of its source candidate's overall quality, yielding improved segmentation. We further build a data pipeline to generate fine-grained multi-round segmentation trajectories and derive supervision for revision actions, candidate selection, and region composition from the model's own outputs, extending training beyond whole-mask IoU supervision. We also propose ReVis-Eval, a benchmark protocol evaluating multi-round revision and cross-round evidence consolidation accuracy. Experiments on ReVis-Eval and 5 benchmarks for remote sensing reasoning and referring segmentation demonstrate the effectiveness of GeoReVis.
