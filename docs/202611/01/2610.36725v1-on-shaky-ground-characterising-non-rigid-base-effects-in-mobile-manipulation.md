## 摘要
移动机器人操作臂将接触任务能力扩展到固定式机器人装置的工作空间之外，但其底座也必须能够承受任务产生的作用力。对于轻型平台，底座运动和支撑结构的柔顺性可能改变工件上实际实现的力与运动。持续接触过程中这些非刚性底座效应如何体现，目前仍缺乏充分表征。本文通过一项打磨案例研究，结合探索性仿真以及在两种轻型、无驱动轮式试验平台上的实验，考察移动机器人操作臂中的非刚性底座效应。对接触力、工具运动和底座振动的联合测量表明，持续接触与显著的任务误差并存：在底座与地面之间阻力较低时，实际实现的力仅为 100 N 指令值的 54%–70%，工具中心点误差可达 100 mm。接触过程中增大底座与地面之间的阻力，可提升力的实现程度并阻止工具进一步位移；而在所测试的条件下，底座振动与力跟踪误差之间并未呈现一致变化。这些观察结果说明，非刚性底座行为会影响接触任务的执行，并凸显了在表征轻型移动操作时综合考察力、运动与振动的价值。

## Abstract
Mobile robot manipulators extend contact-task capability beyond the workspace of fixed robot installations, but their bases must also sustain the forces generated by the task. On lightweight platforms, base motion and support compliance can change the force and motion realised at the workpiece. How these non-rigid base effects manifest during sustained contact remains insufficiently characterised. This paper investigates non-rigid base effects in mobile robot manipulators through a sanding case study combining exploratory simulation with experiments on two lightweight, unactuated wheeled testbeds. Joint measurements of contact force, tool motion, and base vibration show that sustained contact coexisted with substantial task error: under low base-ground resistance, only 54-70% of a 100 N command was realised, with tool-centre-point errors reaching 100 mm. Increasing base-ground resistance during contact improved force realisation and arrested further tool displacement, while base vibration did not vary consistently with force-tracking error in the tested conditions. These observations illustrate how non-rigid base behaviour can affect contact-task execution and highlight the value of examining force, motion, and vibration together when characterising lightweight mobile manipulation.
