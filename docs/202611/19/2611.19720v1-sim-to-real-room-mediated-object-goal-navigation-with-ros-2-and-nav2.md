## 摘要
尽管目标物导航（ObjectNav）方法通常在仿真环境中进行评估，因为仿真能够提供规模化且可重复的环境，但真实世界部署除可行性演示外鲜少受到考察。本文介绍了 Room-Mediated Co-occurrence 的部署：这是一种基于房间概率向量（Room Probability Vectors，RPV）进行推理的 ObjectNav 策略。我们通过与机器人平台无关的 ROS 2 桥接，将该策略部署在两种机器人平台（Boston Dynamics Spot 和 Clearpath Robotics TurtleBot 4）上。我们研究这些语义先验如何泛化至物理环境，并介绍将 RPV 从仿真迁移至硬件所需的适配工作，涵盖传感、定位和动作接口。在 Spot 上进行的 66 次试验中，机器人从大学研究生研究设施内的两个起始位置出发，搜索 11 类物体，取得了 0.455 的原始成功率（SR）。我们的分析指出，未能检测到的玻璃墙、进入限制区域、可用深度范围有限、目标尺寸，以及房间词汇表与实际布局不匹配，可能是现实世界 SR 与已报告的仿真 SR（0.539）之间相差 8.4 个百分点的原因。代码见 https://github.com/Matthew-K-Chua/rpv-ros2-bridge。

## Abstract
Although object-goal navigation (ObjectNav) approaches are usually evaluated in simulation, which offers scale and repeatable environments, real-world deployments are rarely examined beyond feasibility demonstrations. This paper presents the deployment of Room-Mediated Co-occurrence, an ObjectNav policy that reasons over Room Probability Vectors (RPVs), on two robotic platforms (Boston Dynamics Spot and Clearpath Robotics TurtleBot 4) through a robot-agnostic ROS 2 bridge. We study how these semantic priors generalise to physical environments and describe the adaptations required to transfer RPV from simulation to hardware, covering sensing, localisation, and the action interface. Across 66 trials on Spot, searching for 11 object categories from two starting positions in a university postgraduate research facility, RPV achieves a raw Success Rate (SR) of 0.455. Our analysis identifies undetected glass walls, entry into off-limits areas, limited usable depth range, target size, and mismatch between the room lexicon and physical layout as plausible contributors to the 8.4 percentage-point gap between the real-world SR and the reported simulated SR of 0.539. Code is available at https://github.com/Matthew-K-Chua/rpv-ros2-bridge.
