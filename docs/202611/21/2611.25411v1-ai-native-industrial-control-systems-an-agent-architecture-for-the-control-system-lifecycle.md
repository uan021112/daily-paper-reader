## 摘要
工业控制系统日益采用人工智能进行感知、控制与工程设计，但可用的控制功能和执行环境通常预先确定。本文提出一种原生 AI 工业控制系统，由协作智能体根据任务确定所需能力、构建控制应用并准备其执行环境。系统级智能体在已配置的资源与权限约束下协调设备级智能体。本文以一套物理分拣工作站展示两种适应方式。第一种情况下，控制器智能体与机器人智能体在指定的 Raspberry Pi 目标设备上构建分拣应用。第二种情况下，摄像头智能体在边缘节点上增加检测功能，控制器则将检测结果与下游抓取决策关联起来。随后，通过相同的智能体和部署方案进行修订，实现了选择性分拣。试验表明，在该工作站上按任务驱动地构建和演进系统是可行的。调试投运过程也揭示了若干局限，包括设计决策的可追溯性、对委派职责的遵循、诊断推理以及物理行为验证。

## Abstract
Industrial control systems increasingly use AI for perception, control, and engineering, while available control functions and execution environments are typically established in advance. This paper proposes an AI-native industrial control system in which collaborating agents determine the capabilities required by a task, construct the control application, and prepare its execution environment. System-level agents coordinate device-level agents within configured resource and permission constraints. A physical sorting workstation illustrates two forms of adaptation. In the first case, controller and robot agents construct a sorting application on a prescribed Raspberry Pi target. In the second, a camera agent adds inspection on an edge node, and the controller associates inspection results with downstream pickup decisions. A subsequent revision enables selective sorting using the same agents and deployment arrangement. The trials demonstrate the feasibility of task-driven construction and evolution on the workstation. Commissioning also reveals limitations in the traceability of design decisions, adherence to delegated responsibilities, diagnostic reasoning, and verification of physical behavior.
