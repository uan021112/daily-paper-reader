## 摘要
在杂乱环境中，铰接部件的运动不仅受到物体固有的关节运动范围限制，还受到外部障碍物的约束。然而，现有方法主要仅考虑物体的铰接属性。本文提出运动约束预测网络（Motion Constraint Prediction Network，MoCo-Net），这是一种双流神经网络，能够根据部分视角点云观测，同时预测铰接部件的连续运动范围和突出显示潜在碰撞区域的障碍物热图。通过同时捕获内部与外部运动约束，MoCo-Net能够更准确地预测可行运动范围并识别障碍物，提升对新场景中新物体的泛化能力，并使机器人能够构建场景构型空间，从而高效探索物体重排动作。实验表明，MoCo-Net的双流设计能够相互促进，实现更准确的预测；这些预测结果随后构成紧凑的构型空间，供定制化规划器高效地对场景进行重排。

## Abstract
The motion of an articulated part in cluttered surroundings is constrained not only by an object's intrinsic joint limits but also by extrinsic obstacles. However, existing approaches primarily consider object articulation properties alone. Here we present Motion Constraint Prediction Network (MoCo-Net), a two-stream neural network that jointly predicts the continuous motion ranges of articulated parts and obstacle heatmaps highlighting potential collision regions from partial-view point cloud observation. By capturing both internal and external motion constraints, MoCo-Net produces more accurate feasible motion range prediction and obstacles recognition, improves generalization for novel objects in new scenes, and enables robots to construct a scene configuration space for efficient exploration of rearrangement actions. Our experiments show that MoCo-Net's two-stream design enhances each other to deliver more accurate predictions, which subsequently form a compact configuration space for a customized planner to efficiently rearrange the scene.
