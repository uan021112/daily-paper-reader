## 摘要
在真实家庭环境中，辅助机器人必须反复定位从大型家具到桌面微小物品的目标，因为人们会在不同家居表面之间移动物体。对于桌面上的小型物品，将不同尺度的检测结果统一实例化为三维物体可能具有较强脆弱性；如果缺乏显式的物体状态验证，场景发生变化后，过时的条目可能仍会持续存在。我们的核心洞见是，将持久存在的三维家具实体与可修订的表面物体假设分离开来，在无需为每个物体建立独立几何模型的情况下保留其视觉证据。我们将这一设计形式化为假设场景图（Hypothesis Scene Graph，HSG），该图能够保留小物体证据，并支持局部修订，而无需重建稳定的实体拓扑结构。在HSG的基础上，HSGNav利用这种分离机制实现多尺度终身导航：根据目标尺度，检索过程选择实体证据或假设证据；探索过程获取额外的表面观测；验证过程确认目标存在，或使受到矛盾证据支持的假设记录失效。由于现有的终身导航基准（如GOAT-Bench）很少要求在回合中途使记忆失效或进行多尺度搜索，我们提出了FAROS-Bench（Find And Re-find Objects across Scales），用于评估表面物体在回合中途重新放置以及多尺度目标查找任务。实验表明，HSGNav在GOAT-Bench上具有竞争力，同时在FAROS-Bench上显著优于已有方法。

## Abstract
In real homes, assistive robots must repeatedly locate targets from large furniture to tiny tabletop items, as people move objects between household surfaces. Uniformly instantiating detections at different scales as 3D objects can be brittle for small on-surface items; without explicit object-state validation, stale entries may persist after scene changes. Our key insight is to separate persistent 3D furniture entities from revisable surface-object hypotheses, retaining their visual evidence without requiring independent object geometry. We formalize this design as the Hypothesis Scene Graph (HSG), which preserves small-object evidence and supports local revision without rebuilding the stable entity topology. Building on HSG, HSGNav uses this separation for multi-scale lifelong navigation: retrieval selects entity or hypothesis evidence according to target scale, exploration acquires additional surface observations, and verification confirms target presence or invalidates contradicted hypothesis records. Because existing lifelong benchmarks such as GOAT-Bench rarely require mid-episode memory invalidation or multi-scale search, we introduce FAROS-Bench (Find And Re-find Objects across Scales) for mid-episode relocation of surface objects and multi-scale find goals. Experiments show that HSGNav performs competitively on GOAT-Bench while substantially outperforming prior methods on FAROS-Bench.
